Core Taxonomy

The 14 Failure Modes Developers Miss

Date and time bugs consistently rank among the most difficult defects to diagnose. AEVORUN maps code directly to these failure modes.

📅

Month-End & Leap Day

Calendar durations like 1 month are non-uniform (28 to 31 days). Naive addition causes silent phase drift or crashes on February 29th.

⚡

Daylight Saving Shifts

Spring transitions skip an hour (nonexistent local civil times), while autumn transitions repeat an hour (ambiguous times requiring fold=0/1).

🌐

Naive vs. Aware Mismatches

Mixing timezone-naive and timezone-aware objects raises runtime TypeErrors in Python and silently corrupts UTC storage in databases.

⏱️

Timestamp Rounding

Floating-point Unix timestamps lose precision at nanosecond boundaries. Comparisons with equality (==) fail unexpectedly.

🔄

Recurring Schedule Drift

Jobs scheduled by duration drift out of alignment with wall-clock schedules due to leap seconds, clock slewing, and DST adjustments.

🛡️

Zero False Positives

Unlike keyword linters that alert on words like 'delta' in math, AEVORUN-T1 verifies structural temporal context to eliminate noise.
